# Morning Doppio: topic bank

Topics Gašper wants covered from time to time. Use one or two per issue as rotating explainers
(or as the Mental Model / Philosophy / One-liner), researched to the same standard as everything
else. When a topic is used, tick it and add the issue number, e.g. `- [x] Sorites paradox (#04)`.

Fringe or contested topics (marked †) are covered evidence-first: explain the idea, what the
evidence actually says, and why people believe it. Never present them as fact.
