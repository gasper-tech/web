El Herrojo Alto, above La Quinta

Nestled in the elevated hills of La Quinta, Benahavís, El Herrojo Alto is one of the Costa del Sol's most prestigious gated communities — panoramic views across the Mediterranean towards the African coastline, with an atmosphere of quiet exclusivity that is increasingly rare in the Marbella area.

Protected by 24-hour security, with generously sized plots, mature landscaping and a consistently high architectural standard. World-class golf, the Golden Mile and Puerto Banús are all within minutes.
